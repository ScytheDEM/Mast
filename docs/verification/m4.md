## Checklist for a real Australian phone

1. Install the APK; open the map. Expect the explanation text, then the system dialogs for phone and location. Deny once: the bar says "Allow phone access to see your tower" and the map still works. Tap the bar to ask again.
2. With permissions: bar reads like "Telstra 5G · B28 + n78 · -94 dBm · tower 1.3 km NE · Likely · ≈ 85 Mbps". Carrier name, 4G/5G, band, tower distance and direction should match what you can see/know.
3. Tap the bar: the site sheet of that tower opens; the pulse ring and line from you should point to a plausible tower, with faint dots on neighbours.
4. Airplane mode: bar explains. Remove the SIM or switch data SIM: no crash, bar updates.
5. Walk 10 minutes with the map open: Tower data -> Calibration, sample count grows (about one per 50 m / 5 s) and sectors appear; after 3 samples a sector shows "applied".
6. Turn the toggle off: the count stops growing. Turn the map off (home screen): no more samples (battery check).
7. Export CSV: share sheet offers the file. Delete all: confirm, then everything is zero. Reset one sector removes only that card.
8. After a tower data update the calibrated sectors should still be listed (stable sector identity).
