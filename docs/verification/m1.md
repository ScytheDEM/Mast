## Known gaps / deferred

1. **Import restarts from scratch on network change.** During first run the worker was stopped three times (`WM-Processor: WorkerWrapper interrupted ... reason: 7`, i.e. connectivity constraint lost) at 23:35:08, 23:35:29 and 23:38:57, each time restarting at `starting`/`download` and discarding up to 3+ min of import work. The emulator's wifi/mobile networks flapped (default network re-created at 23:35:34), which is realistic on a phone moving between wifi and cellular. Consider resumable download and/or not tying the import (post-download) to the network constraint. Total first-run time was about 10 min instead of about 5 min.
2. **On-device import is slow:** devices phase 3 min 34 s plus 36 s finalise vs 6 s on the JVM. Emulator (debug build, emulated disk/fsync) accounts for much of it; needs a measurement on real hardware and a release build before judging.
3. **No import timing log line** (`AcmaImporter` tag does not exist); timing had to be inferred from WorkManager progress lines.
4. **Disk footprint:** `towers.prev.db` is a full-size copy (88 MB) of the current DB right after the *first* install, so the app holds about 170 MB for an 88 MB database. A rollback copy with nothing to roll back to is wasted space.
5. **My location first tap jumped to Mountain View.** The first tap flew the camera to 37.42,-122.08 (the emulator's stale fused-provider last-known fix, 18 min old) instead of the fresh `geo fix` GPS position; a second tap after re-sending the fix went to Canberra. The emulator default fix makes this partly an artifact, but it indicates last-known selection can prefer a stale fix over a fresher GPS one ("freshest" selection or age cap worth checking).
6. **Compact site sheet appears late after selecting a search result.** After tapping the 101160 search result the camera flew to the site but no sheet was present in the UI dump after about 7 s; it appeared after additional taps on the map dot. In the expanded layout the same flow opened the pane within about 10 s. Emulator screenshots also lagged the UI by several seconds, so this may be latency, but it was not conclusive.
7. **Selected site sits at the edge of the side pane in the expanded layout** ([09](verification/m1/09-expanded-layout.png)): the camera centres the site in the full window, so the marker lies at the pane's left edge rather than centred in the visible map area (cosmetic).
8. **Search "2600" (postcode) returned "No matching sites"** in the one clean attempt (earlier attempts were polluted by repeated typed digits, "26002600"). The site sheet shows "ACT 2600", so postcode data exists; postcode search was not re-verified and may be a defect.
9. Screenshots 09 (about 650 KB) exceeds the 400 KB guidance; others are 80-405 KB.
10. First-run screenshot could not show the idle pre-download state because the import starts automatically on first launch.
